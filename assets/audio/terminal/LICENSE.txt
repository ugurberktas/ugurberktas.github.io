Mechanical keyboard samples
===========================

Source: Keyboard Soundpack #1 [Typing and Single Keystrokes]
Author: unicaegames
Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes
Original files: Single Keys/keypress-001.wav through keypress-005.wav,
keypress-012.wav, keypress-020.wav and keypress-028.wav

The source page publishes the pack under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source readme describes these as recordings of a Cherry KC 1000 keyboard,
recorded with a Shure SM7B and post-processed with Izotope Neutron 2.
